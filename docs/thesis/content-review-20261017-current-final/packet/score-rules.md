# EVCS-THESIS-CONTENT-1.0 assessment rules

This is an unofficial content simulation, not a CDUT score, grade, or blind-review prediction. Use the fixed 12 criteria and maxima in `rubric.json`; the raw maximum is 90. Score in 0.5 increments. Report the raw total as X/90 and the displayed percentage as X/90×100, rounded only after summing. Do not renormalize missing criteria. Every row must cite the current manuscript location, the packet evidence actually inspected, the score rationale, and the remaining gap. School formatting and full-page visual delivery are separate from content scoring.
